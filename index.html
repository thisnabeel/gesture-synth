Gesture Synth Guide

Gesture Synth is an interactive musical instrument created by Eric Wei. Use your hands to create chords, melodies, and sounds in real-time!

Left Hand

Tilt
Inward → Major
Outward → Minor

Fingers (Scale Degree)
1 → I
2 → II
3 → III
4 → IV
5 → V
Index + Pinky → VI
Index + Pinky + Thumb → VII

Progression (+)
Type Nashville numbers like 6-4-1-5 for a practice cheat sheet of left-hand gestures. Minor degrees show an outward tilt cue. While a progression is open, left-hand detection snaps to the nearest step and prefers the next chord when you switch.

Right Hand

Fingers (Chord Quality)
1 → Root Position
2 → 1st Inversion
3 → Major/Minor 7th
4 → Dominant/Diminished 7th

Play Style
Chord → solid voicing
Arpeggio → notes cycle upward through the current voicing at the tapped BPM
TAP → tap the button or Space for arpeggio BPM (one note per beat)
Lock -8ve → pin root-position octave-down voicing; left hand height controls volume

Octave
Thumb In → higher octave
Thumb Out → lower octave

Tilt
Inward → More Filter
Outward → Less Filter

Height
Higher → Louder
Lower → Softer
